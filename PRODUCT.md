# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Requirements | Bean9 serves business analysts, product owners, project managers, developers, and student software teams working inside shared project workspaces. They use it while turning research and stakeholder input into structured BRD, SRS, backlog, and delivery artifacts that the whole team can trace and review.

## Product Purpose

The product provides one source of truth for software requirements, from intent and business context through functional requirements, stories, acceptance criteria, and implementation-facing artifacts. AI assists with analysis and artifact generation, but users remain responsible for review, approval, and project decisions. Success means less requirement drift, clearer ownership, stronger traceability, and a faster path from ambiguous input to delivery-ready work.

## Brand Personality

Structured, focused, trustworthy. The product should feel technically capable and quietly confident: dense enough for serious work, calm enough for long sessions, and clear about what the user can do next.

## Anti-references

- Playful consumer-app styling that makes professional project work feel lightweight.
- Generic neon AI cockpit visuals, excessive glow, or technology theater.
- Decorative glassmorphism, gradient-heavy surfaces, and ornamental SaaS dashboards.
- Inconsistent controls or novel interactions that make standard tasks harder to learn.
- Empty states and AI output that hide uncertainty or leave the next action unclear.

## Design Principles

1. **Structure before decoration.** Make hierarchy, relationships, status, and ownership legible before adding visual flourish.
2. **Keep people in control of AI.** Show what the agent is doing, what it proposes, and where human review is required.
3. **Preserve workflow context.** Navigation, filters, and project state should help users stay oriented across complex artifacts.
4. **Use familiarity to build trust.** Standard controls and consistent interaction patterns should disappear into the task.
5. **Make dense work calm.** Support information-rich screens without visual noise, unnecessary motion, or ambiguous emphasis.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. All workflows must be keyboard accessible, expose clear focus and validation states, preserve readable contrast, avoid color-only meaning, and provide reduced-motion behavior. Vietnamese and English content must remain readable at zoom and across responsive layouts.
